Gourmet Cheesy Fusion Flatbread

timer 25 mins
local_fire_department Medium

The Process

1

Preheat your oven to the highest setting, ideally 500°F (260°C). Place a pizza stone inside to get it scorching hot. This is crucial for that perfect blistered crust.

2

Stretch your dough on a generously floured surface. You want an artisanal, slightly irregular shape. Don't overwork it—keep those air pockets intact.

3

Layer the base heavily with the garlic confit paste, followed by a mountain of our proprietary three-cheese blend. Scatter the exotic spice mix evenly across the top.

4

Slide onto the hot stone and bake for 8-10 minutes until bubbling and charred at the edges. Finish with a heavy handful of fresh green herbs immediately out of the oven.
